OUR TAKE

Reach doesn’t open doors. Recall does.

Most content chases impressions. We build yours around one idea: that a stranger should be able to describe you after a single scroll. Your voice, your point of view, the one thing you stand for.

Numbers follow once people remember you. What we care about is what comes next: real conversations, new clients, and the kind of work you actually want to be doing.

03 — HOW IT WORKS

From first call to a brand people recall.

A clear system, step by step. No guesswork.

STEP 01

Intro call

We learn about you, your work and your goals, review your current profile and tell you honestly what we see.

STEP 02

Story and positioning

We find the story only you can tell, decide how you want to be remembered, then set up your profile around it.

STEP 03

Plan and system

We build your content pillars and a monthly calendar, so every post has a reason to exist.

STEP 04

Scripts and shoot

We write and refine scripts each week, then shoot together in Lisbon and help you feel at ease on camera.

STEP 05

Edit, post and review

We edit, schedule and look at how each piece did. Every week we keep what works and plan the next batch.

a fair warning →

Give it a season.

A personal brand does not form in a fortnight. People need time to notice you, recall you and begin to trust you. That is why we ask for three months. By then there is enough work and enough data to see what is landing, and to do more of it.

05 — WHO RUNS THIS
“I began by sharing everything I knew, and slowly learned that people don’t follow content. They remember people.”

I started out explaining ideas in plain pictures, a few minutes at a time. Years on, people still bring up one clip they saw and remember. That is when I understood what a personal brand really is.

Later I changed direction and started again from zero. It taught me how a brand is built: a real story, a clear message and a simple system.

06 — FAQ

Questions you might have.

Do I need a big audience to start?+

No. You can begin with no followers at all. Once your brand identity is settled, we help you set the account up properly.

How soon will I see results?+

Most clients see steady movement within the first month and a clear pattern by the end of the third.

I am not comfortable on camera. Is that okay?+

That is common. We coach you on camera and write scripts that sound like you.

Can I take just the strategy?+

Yes. Strategy-only work is part of the Guided plan.

Which platform do you work on?+

We work mostly with Instagram and short video, then adapt the system to wherever your audience is.

Where are you based?+

We are based in Lisbon and work with clients remotely or on location.

I am a doctor, lawyer or therapist. Does this fit me?+

Yes. We take extra care with positioning and wording for regulated professions.
