03 — HOW IT WORKS
From first call to a brand people recall.
A clear system, step by step. No guesswork.
STEP 01
Intro call
We learn about you, your work and your goals, review your current profile and tell you honestly what we see.
STEP 02
Story and positioning
We find the story only you can tell, decide how you want to be remembered, then set up your profile around it.
STEP 03
Plan and system
We build your content pillars and a monthly calendar, so every post has a reason to exist.
STEP 04
Scripts and shoot
We write and refine scripts each week, then shoot together in Lisbon and help you feel at ease on camera.
STEP 05
Edit, post and review
We edit, schedule and look at how each piece did. Every week we keep what works and plan the next batch.
